Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our design process, pricing models, and site execution in Birgunj & Parsa.

How much does interior design cost per sq. ft. in Birgunj, Nepal?

Our pricing is completely transparent. For 2D space planning & AutoCAD drafting, rates start at Rs. 25–40 per sq. ft. For full 3D photorealistic visualization and material palettes, rates range between Rs. 60–100 per sq. ft. Complete turnkey execution (including false ceilings, carpentry, electrical, and flooring) ranges between Rs. 1,600–2,800 per sq. ft., depending on whether you choose standard, premium, or luxury finishes.

What is the advantage of hiring a Civil Engineer for interior design?

Traditional interior decorators focus only on surface colors. As a qualified Civil Engineer, I evaluate structural load-bearing walls, concrete soundness, concealed plumbing slopes, and electrical loads. Furthermore, I prepare an exact Bill of Quantities (BOQ), ensuring you purchase the exact material volume without contractor overcharging.

Do you provide 3D photorealistic renders and 360° virtual tours?

Yes! Every project includes high-definition realistic renders and interactive 360° virtual reality panoramas (using SketchUp, Enscape, and V-Ray). This allows you to walk through and experience your exact living room or bedroom on your phone or computer before any construction begins.

How long does the interior design and drafting process take?

Initial conceptual space layouts with at least 3 alternative options are prepared within 3 to 5 business days. Once a layout is approved, photorealistic 3D renders take 5 to 7 days, and complete contractor-ready AutoCAD execution blueprint packages are completed in 10 to 14 business days.

Can you design and execute strictly within my budget?

Yes. Because of our strict Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimation process, we engineer your design backward from your target budget. We specify materials, laminates, lighting fixtures, and sanitary ware that align precisely with your financial boundaries, eliminating unexpected mid-project cost spikes.
